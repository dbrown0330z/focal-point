# Content & pages

Go to **Content & Pages** in the admin sidebar. This area has four tabs.

## Homepage

Edit what members see on the home page after logging in. You can add, remove, and reorder content blocks such as:

- Welcome banner
- Upcoming events widget
- Recent posts feed
- Current competition widget

Changes are saved and reflected immediately on the member home page.

## Pages

Create and manage custom pages that appear in the member navigation. For example:

- *Club history* page under **Our Club**
- *Workshops* page under a custom top-level tab

Each page has a title, a rich-text editor, a visibility setting (Members only / Public), and a navigation location.

## Navigation

Control which pages and sections appear in the member top navigation. You can:

- Show or hide built-in pages (e.g. Calendar, About)
- Create custom top-level navigation tabs
- Add custom sub-pages to any section

## Club galleries

Create and manage curated galleries that appear under **Our Club → Club galleries**. Each gallery can include images from member submissions or direct uploads.

## About page

The **About our club** page has its own editor within Content & Pages. Use the rich-text editor for the prose section and the **side panel** settings to control which club details are shown (location, meeting schedule, social links, etc.).
