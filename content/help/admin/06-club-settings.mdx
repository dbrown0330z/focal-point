# Club settings

Go to **Club Basics** in the admin sidebar. Settings are grouped into several sections.

## General

- **Club name** and short name
- **Location** and contact email
- **Timezone** — used for competition deadline timestamps
- **Logo** — upload your club's logo (shown in the admin header)

## Competition defaults

These are the default rules applied to every new competition. Individual competitions can override them.

- **Submission limits** — max images per member, per category
- **Image size** — required long edge (pixels)
- **Scoring** — min/max score, decimals allowed, aggregation method
- **Results visibility** — immediate, delayed by N hours, or admin-controlled
- **Judge comments** — optional or required, minimum character count

## Points of the year (POY)

Configure how season-long standings are calculated:

- Whether categories count separately
- How many competition scores count per member
- Tiebreaker rules

## Member classes

If your club uses tiered competition classes (e.g. Class A, B, C), define them here. Members can then be assigned a class on their profile.

## Membership terms

Upload your club's membership terms document (PDF or DOCX). Members are shown this during the application process.
