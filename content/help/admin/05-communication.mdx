# Communication

Go to **Communication** in the admin sidebar to send messages to your members.

## Composing a message

1. Click **New message**.
2. Choose your recipients: **All members**, a specific **role** (e.g. Members only), or **individual members**.
3. Enter a **subject** and the **message body**.
4. Click **Send**.

Messages are sent as plain-text emails to each recipient's registered email address. They are not stored in the member portal — members receive them directly in their email inbox.

## Message history

The Communication page shows a log of all previously sent messages including the subject, number of recipients, and the date sent.

> **Tip:** For important announcements that should be visible in the member portal (not just email), use **Posts** instead. Posts appear on the member home page and can be published/unpublished.

## Posts vs. messages

| | Posts | Messages |
|---|---|---|
| Visible in member portal? | Yes | No |
| Sent to email? | No | Yes |
| Can be scheduled? | No | No |
| Stays searchable? | Yes | Log only |
