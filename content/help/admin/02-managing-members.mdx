# Managing members

Go to **Members** in the admin sidebar to see all registered users.

## Approving new members

New sign-ups appear in the **Pending** tab. Click a member's row to review their details, then click **Approve** to grant them member access or **Decline** to reject their application.

> Approved members receive an email notification and can log into the member portal immediately.

## Member roles

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| `null` | Account created but not yet approved — cannot access member portal |
| **Member** | Full member portal access |
| **Admin** | Member portal + full admin area access |

To change a member's role, open their profile in the Members list and use the **Role** dropdown.

## Member classes

If your club uses tiered classes (e.g. Class A, B, C), you can assign a class to each member from their profile page. Classes can be set up under **Club Basics → Member classes**.

## Deactivating a member

Setting a member's status to **Inactive** removes their portal access without deleting their competition history or images.
