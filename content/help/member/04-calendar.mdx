# Calendar & events

The club calendar shows all upcoming meetings, competitions, and events in one place.

## Viewing the calendar

Click **Calendar** in the top navigation. Events are shown in a monthly view.

- **Meetings** — regular club meeting nights with venue and time details
- **Competition deadlines** — when submissions open and close
- **Club events** — outings, workshops, and social events

## Event details

Click any event to see full details including location, description, and any notes the admin has added.

## Competitions on the calendar

Competition dates are added automatically when an admin creates a competition. The calendar will show:

- Submission opens
- Submission deadline
- Judging night (if scheduled)
- Results date
