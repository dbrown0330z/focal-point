# Getting started

Welcome to your club's member portal. Once your membership has been approved by an admin, you'll receive a confirmation email and can log in at any time.

## Logging in

Go to your club's URL and click **Log in**. Enter the email address you used when you applied and the password you set during sign-up.

> **Forgot your password?** Click *Forgot password* on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your email.

## The home page

After logging in you land on the club home page. Depending on how your club admin has set it up, you may see:

- A **welcome message** and recent club news
- Upcoming **calendar events**
- Links to your **latest competition** and image library

## Your profile

Click your **name or avatar** in the top-right corner and select **My profile** to:

- Update your display name and bio
- Upload a profile photo
- Set your member class (if your club uses them)
- Change your password

Keep your profile up to date — your display name appears on competition score sheets and the member directory.
