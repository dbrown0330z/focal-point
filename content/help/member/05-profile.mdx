# Your profile

Your profile stores your name, bio, and display photo. Keeping it up to date helps other members recognise you in the directory.

## Editing your profile

1. Click your **name/avatar** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **My profile**.
3. Update any of the following:
   - **Display name** — shown on score sheets and the member directory
   - **First and last name**
   - **Bio** — a short description visible to other members
   - **Profile photo** — JPG or PNG, at least 200×200 px
   - **Member class** — if your club uses tiered classes

4. Click **Save**.

## Changing your password

On the My profile page, scroll to the **Security** section and click **Change password**. You'll need to enter your current password to confirm.

## Member directory

Your profile (display name and photo) appears in the member directory accessible to all logged-in members under **Our Club → Member directory**. If you'd prefer to keep your profile private, ask your club admin.
