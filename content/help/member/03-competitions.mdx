# Competitions

Each month the club runs a photo competition. Competitions are organised into **categories** (e.g. Open, Nature, Portrait) and judged externally.

## Entering a competition

1. Go to **Competitions** in the top navigation and click the open competition.
2. Choose a **category** to enter.
3. Select an image from your library, or upload a new one on the spot.
4. Click **Submit**.

> **Submission rules** — each image can only be entered in one competition at a time. Your club may also have a limit on how many images you can submit per competition.

## Withdrawing a submission

Changed your mind? Open the competition, find your submission, and click **Withdraw**. This frees the image so it can be re-submitted elsewhere.

## Viewing results

Once judging is complete and the admin publishes the results, go to **Competitions → Results** to see:

- Scores or rankings for each category
- Judge comments (if your club enables them)
- Any special awards (e.g. Best in Show)

Results are visible to all members.

## Points of the year

If your club runs a season-long standings table, your competition scores are tallied automatically. Check **Competitions → Standings** to see your current position.
