# Uploading images

Your image library is personal to you. Images you upload here can be submitted to competitions and grouped into galleries.

## Adding images

1. Click **Images → Upload an image…** in the top navigation.
2. Drag and drop files or click to browse. JPG, PNG, TIFF and HEIC files are supported.
3. Add a **title** (required) and an optional capture date.
4. Click **Upload**.

Your image is processed and stored privately — only you and admins can see it until you submit it to a competition or add it to a gallery.

## My image library

**Images → My images** shows all your uploaded photos in a grid. From here you can:

- **Edit** the title or capture date by clicking the image
- **Delete** an image (this also removes any competition submissions using it)
- **Submit** directly to an open competition

## Galleries

Galleries let you group images for sharing. Go to **Images → My Galleries** to create and manage them.

1. Click **New gallery**, give it a name and optional description.
2. Open the gallery and click **Add images** to pick from your library.
3. Set the gallery visibility: *Private* (only you), *Members only*, or *Public*.

Public galleries appear on the club's public gallery pages.
